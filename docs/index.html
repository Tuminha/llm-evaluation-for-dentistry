Clinician-reviewed specialty benchmark

Which frontier LLMs can answer dental questions safely?

A public benchmark of 30 open-ended dental questions across periodontology, implants, oral-systemic medicine, pharmacology, and patient communication. Every answer, rubric, judge verdict, and analysis script is released.

Explore results Read PDF Read summary Open infographic

Takeaway

The useful signal is clinical, not just numerical.

The top frontier systems are close on a 30-question dataset. The more important distinction is where answers fail: thresholds, guideline nuance, overstatement of evidence, refusals, and judge inconsistency.

Read this as a reproducible benchmark report. Small score gaps are noisy; all headline metrics preserve the stored primary-judge endpoint.
